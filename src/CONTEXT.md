# P.R.O.M.I.S.E. Core Features

## 1. AI-Assisted PCOS Risk Screening

P.R.O.M.I.S.E. provides a rule-based PCOS risk screening supported by artificial intelligence. Rather than diagnosing PCOS, the system evaluates user-provided health information and explains the factors contributing to the overall risk assessment.

### Features
- Rule-based PCOS risk assessment
- AI-assisted explanation of screening results
- Personalized health recommendations
- Risk Assessment Confidence

---

## 2. Intelligent Menstrual Phase Calendar

Unlike conventional menstrual calendars, the P.R.O.M.I.S.E. Calendar continuously analyzes menstrual cycle patterns alongside symptom logs, lifestyle behaviors, dermatologic findings, and AI-assisted risk assessment to provide personalized reproductive health guidance.

### Features
- Automatic menstrual phase detection
- Phase duration monitoring
- Delayed ovulation monitoring
- Prolonged follicular phase detection
- Personalized phase-based recommendations

---

## 3. Daily Phase-Based Health Assistant

The system delivers personalized recommendations based on the user's current menstrual phase.

### Personalized Guidance
- Recommended foods
- Foods to avoid
- Exercise recommendations
- Sleep guidance
- Hydration reminders
- Stress management tips
- Daily wellness notifications

---

## 4. Dermatological Assessment

P.R.O.M.I.S.E. monitors visible dermatologic manifestations commonly associated with hormonal imbalance through AI-assisted image analysis and longitudinal tracking.

### Features
- Acne assessment
- Facial hair assessment
- Skin condition monitoring
- AI image analysis
- Dermatologic trend tracking

---

## 5. Lifestyle Assessment

The application continuously evaluates daily lifestyle behaviors that may influence reproductive health.

### Daily Monitoring
- Sleep quality
- Physical activity
- Nutrition
- Hydration
- Stress levels

These records contribute to personalized recommendations and AI-assisted risk screening.

---

## 6. Symptom Monitoring

Users can record menstrual and reproductive health symptoms throughout each menstrual phase.

### Supported Symptoms
- Abdominal pain
- Bloating
- Fatigue
- Mood changes
- Acne
- Headache
- Cramping
- Food cravings
- Menstrual flow
- Breast tenderness

The system analyzes recurring symptom patterns over time.

---

## 7. AI Educational Chatbot

An AI-powered educational assistant provides evidence-based reproductive health education tailored to the user's menstrual phase and recorded symptoms.

### Capabilities
- Menstrual health education
- PCOS awareness
- Personalized explanations
- Self-care recommendations
- Phase-specific guidance
- Lifestyle education

---

## 8. Doctor Visit Report Generator

P.R.O.M.I.S.E. automatically summarizes clinically relevant observations into an organized report that users may share with healthcare professionals.

### Report Includes
- Menstrual history
- Cycle trends
- Dermatologic observations
- Lifestyle trends
- Symptom history
- AI-generated observations
- PCOS risk screening history

---

## 9. Explainable AI Insights

Rather than presenting only a risk category, the AI explains the reasoning behind each screening result.

### Example Explanation

**Moderate Risk**

Reason:
- Irregular menstrual cycles observed
- Persistent acne
- Elevated BMI
- Lifestyle factors
- Positive family history (if provided)

This improves transparency and helps users better understand the factors contributing to their screening results.

---

## 10. Risk Assessment Confidence

To improve transparency and responsible AI use, P.R.O.M.I.S.E. displays a **Risk Assessment Confidence** score that reflects the reliability of the generated screening result based on the completeness and consistency of the available health information.

**Note:** This confidence score represents the reliability of the screening assessment—not the probability that the user has PCOS.

### Example 1

**Risk Screening Result:** Moderate Risk

**Risk Assessment Confidence:** **92% (High Confidence)**

Reason:
- Six months of menstrual history
- Complete dermatological assessments
- Daily lifestyle records
- Consistent symptom tracking
- Minimal missing information

---

### Example 2

**Risk Screening Result:** Moderate Risk

**Risk Assessment Confidence:** **41% (Low Confidence)**

Reason:
- Limited menstrual history
- Missing lifestyle records
- No dermatological assessment
- Incomplete symptom tracking

The system encourages users to continue recording health information to improve the reliability of future screening results.

---

## 11. Cross-Module Intelligent Integration

All major modules within P.R.O.M.I.S.E. communicate with one another, allowing health information collected from different components to contribute to a more comprehensive reproductive health assessment.

```text
Calendar
     │
Lifestyle Assessment
     │
Symptom Monitoring
     │
Dermatological Assessment
     │
AI Risk Screening
     │
Educational Chatbot
     │
Doctor Visit Report
```

This interconnected design enables personalized recommendations and a more holistic understanding of the user's reproductive health.

---

## 12. Personalized Health Insights

P.R.O.M.I.S.E. continuously analyzes longitudinal health records to identify meaningful trends and provide proactive wellness insights.

### Example Insights

- Your sleep quality has decreased over the past two weeks while stress levels have increased.
- Your acne commonly worsens before prolonged menstrual cycles.
- Your symptoms have improved compared to last month.
- Your menstrual cycles have become more regular over the last three months.
- Your hydration habits have improved, which may contribute to better overall wellness.

These personalized observations help users better understand their reproductive health patterns while supporting informed discussions with healthcare professionals.
