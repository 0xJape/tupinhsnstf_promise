D. Symptom Trend Analysis

E. Connect With Menstrual Tracking

SAFE WORDING: “You recorded acne during your recent menstrual period.” Do not state that the menstrual cycle caused the symptom.

PURPOSE: Transform individual symptom logs into an easy-to-understand personal health journey, helping users monitor changes, recognize patterns in their own records, prepare questions, and communicate more clearly during consultation.

 
SAFETY BOUNDARY: The Symptom Journey Map is a tracking and visualization tool, not a diagnostic tool. It should not determine the medical cause of symptoms or claim that a symptom confirms PCOS.

 
9. PCOS Manifestation Explorer
Add a structured visual educational module that helps users understand the different ways PCOS may manifest.
Four Major Areas
Menstrual / Reproductive — menstrual and reproductive manifestations associated with PCOS
Hormonal — hormonal-related manifestations
Dermatologic — examples such as acne, excess hair growth, and hair thinning
Metabolic — metabolic manifestations associated with PCOS
Briefly explain what each manifestation means, how it may relate to PCOS, and why PCOS can present differently among individuals.
IMPORTANT: Experiencing one manifestation does not automatically mean that a person has PCOS. This module is for education and awareness, not self-diagnosis.
